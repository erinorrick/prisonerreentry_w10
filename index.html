Graduate Extension

Using Angwin et al. (2016), Larson et al. (2016), and Flores, Bechtel, & Lowenkamp (2016), engage with the methodological debate over ProPublica's COMPAS analysis. You just built the false-positive/false-negative trade-off yourself in Part B: use that firsthand experience to evaluate the argument on each side. Who has the stronger case, and what are the implications for how instruments like ORAS and COMPAS should be used? Note that Flores, Bechtel, and Lowenkamp state explicitly that they are not advocating for COMPAS or Northpointe. They raise five concerns about ProPublica's analysis; the first is that ProPublica tested COMPAS on pretrial defendants when, in their account, its general and violent recidivism scales were designed for people on post-disposition supervision.
